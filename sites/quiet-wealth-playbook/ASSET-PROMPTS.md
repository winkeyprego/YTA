# New Alicia artwork and product images

Prompts for the next round of website assets. Each one says where it will go on the page and what to name the file. Generate them one at a time in the same image tool used for the existing poses.

## Before you generate

**Attach two images to every prompt:**
1. `source/assets/alicia-reference.jpg` (her identity)
2. `source/assets/alicia-shh-v3.png` (the drawing style already on the site)

**Keep the current style.** New poses must sit next to the five existing ones without looking like a different artist drew them. Same clean dark outlines, flat colour with light shading, same proportions.

**Check each result before saving it:**
- Same face, hair, earrings, necklace, navy blazer and white blouse
- Five fingers on every hand; hands holding things properly
- Real transparent background (no white box, no checkerboard painted in)
- Nothing cut off at the edges: hair, hands and feet all inside the frame
- No text, numbers or logos anywhere, including on books and screens

**Save as PNG**, at least 1536px on the long side, into `source/assets/` with the file name given below. Or simply upload them in this chat.

## Shared description

Every prompt below starts with this paragraph. Paste it in first.

> Use the attached reference images as the exact identity and drawing style for Alicia. Keep the same adult woman: warm medium-brown skin, dark brown side-parted bob just above the shoulders, large expressive dark eyes, small gold stud earrings, thin gold necklace, tailored navy blazer over a white open-collared blouse. Match the existing artwork exactly: polished 2D editorial cartoon, clean confident dark outlines, flat fills with soft restrained shading, the same facial proportions. One character only. Genuinely transparent background with real alpha, no scenery, no floor, no shadow shape, no glow, no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Natural anatomy with five fingers on each hand. Generous empty transparent padding on every side so nothing touches the edge.

## Product images (the biggest upgrade)

These don't need image generation. Even rough drafts help, and the page will label them honestly. Send what you have:

1. **The final cover**, as a flat front-facing image (PNG or JPG, 1200px or larger)
2. **Four to six interior pages** of the Playbook: an opening chapter page, a page with Jess or Tanya, a tips page, a summary page
3. **A workbook screenshot**: one calculator in Excel or Google Sheets, with example numbers
4. **One script page** and **one decision tree**
5. **One or two phone wallpapers**

When they arrive they go into `source/assets/product/` and are listed under `productSamples` in `content.json` (the README explains how). That shows a "Look inside" section of real pages, kept separate from the free website examples.
