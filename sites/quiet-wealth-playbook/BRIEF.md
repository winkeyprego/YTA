## 1. The job

Build a single-page sales site for **The Quiet Wealth Playbook** (slogan: **The Boring Money System**), a digital value pack sold by the YouTube channel Alicia Invests. The page has one goal: get a visitor from a YouTube description link to a completed purchase. Everything on the page either builds trust or moves them toward the Buy button.

The last version of this product failed mainly because the page and funnel were weak. This one must feel premium, calm and trustworthy, like a well-designed book from a serious publisher, never like a hype funnel.

## 2. Who lands here

- US adults, roughly half men and half women, mostly 30 to 60+.
- They arrive from a YouTube video description or pinned comment, almost always on a phone.
- They already know and like Alicia, an animated character who narrates the channel. They trust her calm, math-first, no-shame teaching style.
- Many are stressed about money. Some are in real debt. The page must never make them feel judged or pressured.

## 3. Brand and voice

- **Voice:** warm, plain, confident, specific. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. Alicia speaks in first person in a few places.
- **Never:** hype words (secret, hack, crush, explode), fake urgency, countdown timers that reset, invented testimonials or reviews, income claims, or "as seen on" logos that are not real.
- **Writing rules:** no em dashes anywhere in the copy. Avoid "it's not X, it's Y" sentence patterns.
- **Required line in the footer and near the price:** "Educational content only. Not personal financial, legal or tax advice."

## 4. Visual direction

Aim: **quiet luxury meets a well-made book.** Sleek, spacious, editorial, calm.

- **Palette:** warm off-white background (#FAF7F2 or similar), deep navy for text and primary buttons (match Alicia's navy blazer, about #1B2A41), one muted accent (soft gold #C9A54B or sage #8FA58B), a light warm gray for cards. Provide a dark section or two for contrast, never a full dark theme.
- **Type:** an elegant serif for headlines (for example Fraunces, Playfair Display or Libre Caslon from Google Fonts) and a clean sans for body (Inter or similar). Large, generous line height. Body text at least 18px on mobile.
- **Layout:** lots of white space, a narrow readable column for copy (about 640px), wider for product visuals. Soft rounded corners (8 to 16px), very subtle shadows, thin hairline dividers.
- **Motion:** gentle fade and rise on scroll, a slow hover lift on cards and buttons. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Nothing bouncy or flashy.
- **Imagery:** the Alicia character art (2D illustrated, Black woman with a bob, navy blazer, gold earrings), clean product mockups of the book cover, workbook screenshots and printable pages. Use placeholders with correct aspect ratios until real assets arrive.
- **Feel test:** if a screenshot of the page could pass for a page from a premium publisher or a design-led fintech, it is right. If it looks like a typical internet marketing funnel, it is wrong.

## 6. Checkout and tracking

- **Checkout:** Lemon Squeezy. Every Buy button opens the Lemon Squeezy checkout overlay for [LEMON_SQUEEZY_CHECKOUT_URL] using their official embed script (`lemon.js`). If the script fails to load, the button must still work as a normal link to the checkout URL.
- **UTM passthrough:** read `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` from the page URL and append them to the checkout URL, so sales can be traced to the exact YouTube video.
- **Analytics:** include a placeholder for a privacy-friendly analytics snippet ([ANALYTICS_SNIPPET], for example Plausible). Fire events on: Buy button click (with button location), FAQ open, carousel interaction, and email signup.
- **No cookie banner needed** if using cookieless analytics; otherwise include a minimal, unobtrusive one.

## 7. Technical requirements

- **Stack:** a static site. Prefer plain HTML, CSS and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript, or Astro if you want components. No heavy frameworks, no build step required to deploy if plain HTML is chosen.
- **Hosting target:** deployable as-is to Netlify, Vercel or Cloudflare Pages.
- **Mobile first:** design at 375px wide first, then tablet and desktop. No horizontal scroll at any width.
- **Performance:** Lighthouse 95+ on Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO on mobile. Images in WebP or AVIF with width and height set, lazy-loaded below the fold. Fonts loaded with `font-display: swap`, preconnect to Google Fonts. Total JS under 30KB excluding the Lemon Squeezy script.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA. Real buttons and links, visible focus states, alt text on every image, color contrast checked, accordion and carousel usable by keyboard and screen reader.
- **SEO and sharing:** title "The Quiet Wealth Playbook | Alicia Invests", a meta description, Open Graph and Twitter card tags with a share image placeholder (1200x630), favicon, and JSON-LD `Product` schema with the price.
- **Content in one place:** keep all copy, prices, dates and URLs in a single config object or data file at the top, so they can be changed without touching layout code.
- **Legal pages:** create simple placeholder pages for Privacy Policy, Terms and Refund Policy with clearly marked placeholder text for the owner to replace.
